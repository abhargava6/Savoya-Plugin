## Audience covers (`personalization.cover`)
Pre-designed by marketing; shipped in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/quote-document/assets/covers/`. Pick the one the *buyer* would recognize themselves in.

| Key | Headline | Use when |
|---|---|---|
| `general` | The Trusted Ground Transportation Partner For Your Organization | Default; mixed or unknown buyer |
| `executive-assistants` | …For Executive Assistants | EA / chief of staff booking for executives |
| `event-planners` | …For Meetings & Events Planners | Offsites, conferences, planner-run weddings |
| `high-demand-events` | Transportation Built For High-Demand Events | Summit-scale, multi-city, VIP guest programs |
| `security-teams` | …For Security Teams | Executive protection, duty-of-care buyers |
| `flight-departments` | …For Flight Departments | Corporate aviation, FBO ground |
| `athletic-recruiters` | …For Athletic Recruiters | College athletics visits and staff moves |
| `custom` | (generated from event name + hero image) | User has a hosted image or wants the event on the cover |

Covers ship as pre-rendered JPEGs (2:3, fitted to page height on black). To add one: put `<key>.pdf` in the plugin's `covers-src/`, add a row to `catalog.json`, run `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/quote-document/scripts/make_covers.py covers-src` once. Never rasterize at quote time — it costs ~20s.

## Rules
- **Images must be hosted `https://` URLs** (Cloudinary preferred). Local paths and Drive links break in email and in docx conversion. If the user hands you a file, tell them to upload it and give you the URL; don't embed base64 in email.
- **Intro note — what good looks like.** Reference the event and the group, say what Savoya is handling, one line on what to expect. Example: *"Thanks for bringing the Yerdle leadership team to Boston. We'll have three coaches staged at the Hyatt Regency for the Wednesday harbor dinner and two ready for Friday's departure to Logan. Your on-site coordinator will text you when vehicles are in position."*
- **Pull quotes are the client's words or the user's — never invented.** If the user wants one and doesn't have one, offer to leave it out rather than draft a fake testimonial.
- **Keep the copper accent for personalization elements** so they read as designed, not pasted in.

## Brainstorm menu (for when the user asks "what could I add?")
- Hero photo of the venue or the vehicle class they're getting
- Client logo paired with Savoya's on the cover
- Short welcome note from the account manager
- Event name and dates as the document subtitle
- A testimonial from a similar client (with permission)
- On-site coordinator contact card for day-of
- Per-leg operational notes (staging location, load time, luggage handling)
- Map snapshot of the route (hosted image; not generated by this skill)
- "What's included" strip: chauffeur, wait time, on-site coordination, live tracking via the app
- Closing line personalized to the destination
