# Terms (verbatim — every document includes these unchanged)

A bullet prefixed `[if:fuel_surcharge]` appears only when the quote carries a fuel surcharge line (engine-applied or user-added, and not waived).

A bullet prefixed `[if:commission]` appears only when a commission rate was recorded for the quote. It states the rate and names no beneficiary, because the commission may be earned by the client or by an agency acting for them, and the document should not assert which. It is informational: no commission figure is calculated and no total changes.

- Our pricing includes all charges EXCLUDING: variable fees such as parking, airport fees, tolls, and state taxes where applicable. We bill these charges at cost as they are incurred.
- Our drivers are well compensated and additional gratuity is not expected.
- [if:fuel_surcharge] A fuel surcharge of {pct}% is currently in effect.
- [if:commission] A {pct}% commission applies to this program.
- Transfer rates include up to 30 minutes of complimentary wait time. Additional wait time is billed in 15 minute increments at the market hourly rate. Partial quarter hour increments are rounded to the next quarter hour.
- Trips with stops are billed hourly. Hourly trips are billed garage to garage.
- We have a 2-hour cancellation policy unless otherwise noted above.
- This is a quoting tool and therefore builds quotes. This is not a final price nor is it guaranteed pricing.
