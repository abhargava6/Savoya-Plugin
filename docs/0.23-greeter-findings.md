# Greeter pricing — the as-directed shape works, and bills more than you asked for

Run 7 Oct 2026, profile **201890 (Ayush TC / Yerdle)**, Orange County, 2027-03-20.
Two `calculate_quote_units` calls, greeter `vehicle_type_id: 12`, pickup at the Irvine
Marriott + an as-directed Irvine stop, with only `duration` changed between them.

**Closes Q9's mechanism question. Opens one new question about what the CSM is told.**

---

## The structure works — `duration` is honoured on an as-directed stop

| `duration` sent | `billable_hours` returned | Total |
|---|---|---|
| **2** | **3.25** | $250.22 |
| **4** | **5.25** | $404.20 |

Both priced cleanly: rate 55549 `HourlyRate`, $100.00/hr rack → **$76.99/hr** effective
(30% discount, 10% fuel), 2-hour minimum, 2-hour cancellation. Total is exactly
`billable_hours × effective rate` in both cases.

This confirms the recommendation. The earlier failure — `duration: 4` on a *dropoff* stop
collapsing to the 2-hour minimum — was the wrong stop shape, not a greeter limitation.
`duration` applies to intermediate and as-directed stops; a greeter is
**pickup + as-directed with a duration**.

It also fixes the semantic problem: the hotel → venue shape reported a 13-minute drive for
someone standing in a lobby. The as-directed shape reports **5 minutes**, which is still
not nothing — see below.

## ▲ Core adds 1.25 hours, and no local arithmetic would have predicted it

The delta is constant: **+1.25 h** at both durations. Ask for 4 hours, the client is billed
5.25. Almost certainly positioning or garage-to-garage time, which is Core's business logic
and not ours to reverse-engineer.

**Two consequences, and the first is a user-flow decision:**

1. **The CSM asks for 4 hours and the client sees 5.25.** We send what they asked for and
   display what Core billed — never back-solve `duration = requested − 1.25`, which is
   exactly the local arithmetic this release removes. But a CSM who types "4 hours" and
   gets a 5.25-hour line will think it's a bug unless the reply says why. **This needs
   wording, and it is the kind of thing your pending workflow feedback may touch.**
2. **It is hard evidence for C5.** A 31% gap between requested and billable hours is not
   something any plugin-side reconstruction would have arrived at. Copy Core's numbers;
   never rebuild them.

## ◆ A second greeter mechanism exists and we cannot reach it

Every quote response carries these on the `quote` object, null here:

```
"adjusted_greeter_fee": null,  "greeter_fee": null,
"greeter_fee_in_vendor_currency": 0,  "adjusted_greeter_fee_in_vendor_currency": 0
```

So Core can attach a **greeter fee to an ordinary vehicle's quote**, distinct from pricing a
greeter as its own unit. Nothing in the nine tool schemas sets it — I checked
`calculate_quote_units`, `calculate_temporary_quote_units` and `generate_quote_options`.

That is probably where the $940 was *meant* to go: a fee on the car, not a separate line.
The vehicle-unit path works and prices correctly, so D1 is buildable as designed — but we
should know whether the fee field is the intended route before building the other one.
**This is a question for Ethan, and I have not added it to `ENGINEERING.md`** — say the word
and I will, with both responses as evidence.

## Smaller findings

- **`limited_availability: true`** on the greeter type. We don't read the field (B2 adds it).
  For a greeter it plausibly means "not always staffable", which is worth surfacing before a
  CSM promises one.
- **`estimated_drive_minutes: 5`** still appears on the as-directed greeter leg, in both the
  raw stops and the `presentation` block. Five minutes is less wrong than thirteen, but a
  greeter does not drive anywhere. **The renderer should suppress drive time on an
  as-directed greeter leg** — a client document showing "5 min drive" for a person meeting a
  flight is the kind of detail that gets noticed.
- **`capacity_adjusted: false`, `required_quantity: 1`** — capacity is `null` on greeter
  types, so Core does not try to size a fleet. Passing `passenger_count: 1` was accepted
  without complaint; a greeter unit should not carry the group headcount.
- **The `presentation` block is complete enough to render from** (B8). `view: "quote_result"`
  carried the title, severity-tagged warnings, an `actions` entry (`refine_quote`), and
  per-unit rows with stops, drive minutes and a full pricing object. Nothing we currently
  display was missing from it.
