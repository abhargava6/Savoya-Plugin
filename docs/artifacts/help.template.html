How a quote goes

You are asked a question only where a person has to decide. Everything else runs on its own.

a question for you something you receive a place you can stop 
flowchart TD
    A["Paste the client's request"] --> BQ{"Anything unclear?"}
    BQ -->|yes| C["Answer one question
headcount · which hotel · which year"]
    BQ -->|no| D
    C --> D(["Rate sheet first,
or straight to the quote?"])
    D --> E(["Best value, options,

    E --> SHORT{"Enough vehicles

    SHORT -->|no| NEAR(["Three nearest markets
and what each can add.
You choose."])
    SHORT -->|yes| F
    NEAR --> F["Priced quote
every option, every leg"]
    F --> GQ(["One question: customize it,
go on to the client document,
or just the quote?"])
    GQ -->|customize| F
    GQ -->|just the quote| DONE1(["Done. You have the number"])
    GQ -->|client document| J(["Six quick questions"])
    J --> K["PDF and or email copy"]
    K --> LQ{"Anything you'd like changed?"}
    LQ -->|yes, fix it| K
    LQ -->|no| DONE2(["Done"])

If something looks wrong

Two steps, so the team can see exactly what happened.

1. Tell the assistant:

Log this session. <what looked wrong>

It saves a record of the session, with or without a document.

2. Post a short note in the #core-ideas Slack channel so someone picks it up.
